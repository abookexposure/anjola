Case Study 01

From Page 47 to Page 1: A Romance Novel's Remarkable Transformation

Sarah Mitchell had spent two years writing her debut romance novel, "Whispers in the Wind." She invested in a professional cover, hired an editor, and published through KDP with high hopes. Six months later, she was selling fewer than 15 copies per month and her book was buried on page 47 for her main keyword.

The Challenge: Sarah's book had a beautiful cover and glowing reviews (4.7 stars from 85 reviews), but it was completely invisible in Amazon search. Her keywords were generic and overly competitive ("romance novel," "love story"), her categories were too broad (Fiction > Romance > General), and her book description was a bland summary that failed to hook potential readers.

The Strategy: I conducted a comprehensive 50-point audit of her listing and identified 12 critical issues. I replaced her generic keywords with 35 highly specific long-tail phrases like "enemies to lovers small town romance" and "second chance romance clean." I repositioned her into 5 niche subcategories including Contemporary Romance > Small Town and Romance > Clean & Wholesome. I rewrote her description with a compelling hook, emotional bullet points, and a strong call-to-action. Finally, I designed premium A+ Content with 5 modules showcasing reader reviews, character previews, and a "If You Love..." comparison section.

The Results: Within 3 weeks, her book jumped from page 47 to page 1 for her primary keyword. Within 8 weeks, she earned the #1 bestseller badge in Contemporary Romance > Small Town. Monthly sales increased from 15 to over 600 copies — a 4,000% increase. Sarah has since hired me to optimize her second and third books, both of which reached bestseller status within their first month.

Case Study 02

Scaling a 12-Book Children's Series from Obscurity to Dominance

Emily Chen had published 12 beautifully illustrated children's books in her "Luna's Magical Garden" series over three years. Despite rave reviews from parents and teachers, the series was averaging only 200 total monthly sales across all 12 titles. She was ready to give up on self-publishing entirely.

The Challenge: The children's book market on Amazon is incredibly competitive, with major publishers dominating the top spots. Emily's books were all categorized under the same broad "Children's Books > Fiction" category, competing directly against Disney and Scholastic titles. Her keywords were too generic ("children's book," "bedtime story"), and none of her books had A+ Content. The series lacked cross-linking, so readers who loved one book had no easy way to discover the others.

The Strategy: I developed a comprehensive series-wide optimization plan. For each of the 12 books, I identified 25+ niche-specific keywords like "magical garden fairy tale ages 4-8" and "nature adventure picture book girls." I repositioned each book into unique subcategories to avoid internal competition — spreading them across Fairy Tales, Nature, Friendship, and Imagination subcategories. I created custom A+ Content for all 12 books with series cross-promotion modules that linked readers from one book to the next. I also optimized the series page and added "Read the Full Series" CTAs to every description.

The Results: Total series sales jumped from 200 to over 2,500 monthly copies — a 1,150% increase. Three individual titles earned bestseller badges in their respective subcategories. The series page now ranks on page 1 for "magical garden children's books." Emily's monthly royalty income increased by over $8,000, and she's now planning books 13 through 18 with confidence.

1,150% Sales Increase From 200 to 2,500+ monthly series sales
12 Books Optimized Complete series overhaul with cross-linking
+$8,000/Month Additional monthly royalty income generated
Series Growth Author expanding from 12 to 18 books
Case Study 03

Quadrupling Revenue for a Business Book with A+ Content & Metadata

David Park is a successful startup founder who wrote "Startup Scaling Secrets" to share his entrepreneurial journey. The book had solid content and a loyal following on social media, but his Amazon listing was converting at only 3.2% — meaning 97 out of every 100 visitors left without buying.

The Challenge: David's book description was a wall of plain text with no formatting, no hooks, and no emotional appeal. His A+ Content section was completely empty. His title was too vague to rank for any meaningful keywords, and his backend search terms were filled with irrelevant phrases like "business" and "money" that were far too competitive to rank for.

The Strategy: I completely overhauled David's Amazon listing. I optimized his subtitle to include high-impact keywords: "Startup Scaling Secrets: The Founder's Playbook for Growing from $0 to $10M in Revenue." I rewrote the description with HTML formatting, emotional hooks, bullet-point benefits, and reader testimonials. I designed 7 custom A+ Content modules including a "What You'll Learn" section, author bio with credibility markers, comparison chart vs. competitors, and a "Praise for This Book" review showcase. I replaced all backend keywords with specific phrases like "startup growth strategy" and "entrepreneur scaling playbook."

The Results: The conversion rate jumped from 3.2% to 11.8% — a 269% improvement. Monthly revenue quadrupled within 60 days. The book now ranks on page 1 for 8 targeted keywords and has maintained a consistent top-50 position in the Startup & Entrepreneurship category. David credits the A+ Content alone with generating an additional $12,000 in revenue in the first quarter after implementation.
