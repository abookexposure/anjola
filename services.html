Service 03

Amazon Category Optimization

Category placement is one of the most overlooked yet powerful levers for Amazon success. Most authors choose their categories randomly during upload and never revisit them — leaving massive opportunities on the table.

Amazon has over 16,000 categories and subcategories, but KDP only lets you select two during upload. The secret most authors don't know is that you can request additional category placements through Amazon Author Support — and I know exactly which ones to request. I analyze the competitive density of thousands of categories to find the sweet spots where your book can realistically reach the top 100 and earn that coveted orange bestseller badge.

My category optimization service includes a complete analysis of your current placements, identification of optimal new categories, preparation of the exact request language to send to Amazon support, and a strategic roadmap for earning bestseller badges in the most achievable categories for your genre.

16,000+ Categories Analyzed Deep analysis of Amazon's full category tree to find your perfect niches
Bestseller Badge Strategy Targeted path to earning the #1 bestseller badge in achievable categories
Done-For-You Requests Pre-written Amazon support request templates ready to copy and paste
BISAC Code Mapping Hidden category codes that unlock placements most authors never discover

Each A+ Content project includes custom graphic design for up to 7 modules, persuasive copywriting for every section, mobile-responsive layout optimization, and full implementation on your Amazon listing. The result is a stunning sales page that looks like it was created by a major publishing house — at a fraction of the cost.
